## How I think about AI products

- **Start with the workflow and business outcome**, not the model or the demo.
- Choose the right mechanism—deterministic rules, an LLM, an agent or a human—based on uncertainty, risk and economics.
- Evaluate end-to-end quality with explicit ground truth, human oversight and measurable acceptance criteria.
- Optimise for the real unit economics: cost per successfully completed task, not just cost per token or model call.
- Treat reliability, governance, adoption and operating-model change as part of the product.
